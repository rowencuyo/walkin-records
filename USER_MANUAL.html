1. Getting Started

Logging In

When you open the application, you will be greeted by the secure login screen. To access the system:

  1. Enter your registered Username.
  2. Enter your Password.
  3. Click Login or press Enter.
📝 First-Time Users: The default administrator credentials are set during the initial installation. Please contact your IT administrator if you do not have your login details.

Security Lock Screen

To protect sensitive student data, the application features an automatic lock mechanism. If you leave the application idle for a specific duration (configurable in Settings), it will temporarily lock the screen.

  • To resume work, simply type your password. You do not need to re-enter your username.
  • You can adjust the idle timeout or disable the auto-lock entirely in the Settings panel.

2. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is your control center. It provides an immediate, high-level overview of the most critical information requiring your attention.

Key Metrics Cards

At the top of the dashboard, you will find six colored metric cards. Clicking any of these cards will instantly take you to the Records list, pre-filtered to show exactly those students.

Metric Card Description & Action
Active Records The total number of currently enrolled or active students in the system.
Inactive Records Students who have graduated, withdrawn, or had their records archived.
Missing Documents Students whose profiles are incomplete (e.g., missing passports or visas).
Expired Visas Students currently holding a visa that has already passed its validity date.
Expiring Soon Visas that are getting close to their expiration (default warning is 30 days).
Not Updated (90d) Records that have not been modified or reviewed in the last 3 months.

System Alerts & Notifications

Below the cards, the Alerts panel lists the top 5 most urgent notifications (such as visas expiring tomorrow). Click the Open button next to an alert to jump directly to the affected student's profile.

Visual Trends & Activity

  • Record Trends: A line chart visualizing how many new records were added over the last 6 months. This helps identify enrollment spikes.
  • Recent Activity: A quick-access list of the last 10 records you interacted with. Perfect for jumping right back into where you left off.

3. Managing Records

The Records page is where you will spend most of your time browsing, filtering, and organizing student data.

Viewing Modes

You can toggle how student data is displayed using the view buttons at the top right:

  • Cards View (Default): Displays modern, visual cards for each student, highlighting their photo, passport number, and visa status.
  • Table View: A dense, spreadsheet-style list. Ideal for power users who want to see maximum data on screen and sort by specific columns.

Searching & Precision Filtering

Quickly locate any student using the top toolbar:

  1. Global Search: Type a name, passport number, or country into the search bar. The results will update instantly as you type.
  2. Filters dropdowns: Narrow down your list by selecting specific criteria such as Visa Status, Educational Level, Year Level, or Active/Archived state.

Opening & Interacting with Records

  • Single Click: Opens a sliding "Preview Panel" on the right side of the screen, allowing you to peek at the details without leaving your list.
  • Double Click: Opens the full, comprehensive Student Profile view.

Batch Updating (Table View Only)

Need to update the status of an entire class at once? Use the Batch Update tool:

  1. Switch to the Table View.
  2. Hold Ctrl (or Shift for a range) and click multiple rows to select them.
  3. A blue action toolbar will slide up from the bottom of the screen.
  4. Select the new Enrollment Status from the dropdown menu.
  5. Click Apply. All selected students will be updated simultaneously!

4. Adding & Editing Records

Whether you are creating a new student record (by clicking + Add Record) or editing an existing one, the application uses a unified, structured form.

Form Organization

To prevent information overload, the form is divided into logical categories:

  • Identity: Core metrics like Name, Sex, Date of Birth, Passport Number, and Country of Citizenship.
  • Philippine Residential Address: The student's local geographic details.
  • Academic & Residency: Start dates, educational program, academic level, and remarks.
  • Visa Information: Critical immigration tracking, including Visa Category, Grant Date, and Validity Date.
⚠️ Required Fields: Fields marked with an asterisk (*) must be filled out before you can save. If you miss a required field, the form will highlight it in red when you attempt to save.

Intelligent Autosave

Never lose your work due to a power outage or accidental click.

  • The form automatically saves an internal draft every 30 seconds as you type.
  • If the application closes unexpectedly, the next time you try to edit or add a record, a prompt will ask if you want to Restore your Draft or Discard it.
  • If you try to manually leave the page without saving, the system will actively block you and ask if you want to Save, Discard, or Cancel your navigation.

5. Student Profiles

The Student Profile is the central hub for a single individual. It contains all their text data, attached documents, and operational controls.

Visual Indicators

  • Profile Picture: Click the generic avatar to upload a photo of the student. Supported formats include JPG, PNG, and WebP (up to 5 MB). You can also click to remove or change the photo later.
  • Completeness Banner: If the student's profile is missing required data or mandatory documents, a bright orange banner sits at the top outlining exactly what is missing.
  • Visa Countdown Badge: Immediately know the student's legal status:

    🟢 Green More than 30 days remaining on their visa.
    🟡 Amber 30 days or less remaining (Action required soon).
    🔴 Red Visa is expired!

Document Management

The system acts as a digital filing cabinet for the student.

  1. Click + Upload Document.
  2. Select the type of document (e.g., Passport, Birth Certificate, Graduation Certificate).
  3. Choose the file from your computer (PDFs or Images, up to 25 MB).

Missing Files: If a linked document is moved or deleted from your actual computer folder outside the application, the app will show a red !! MISSING FILE warning. You can click Locate to re-link it.

Deleting vs. Restoring

Instead of permanently destroying data, deleting a record "Soft Deletes" it. It removes the student from active lists, but they can be fully recovered later by navigating to their profile (via the Inactive Records filter) and clicking Restore.

6. Notifications & Alerts

The system automatically tracks expiring visas and unresolved missing documents, generating notifications so nothing slips through the cracks.

The Notifications center is split into three tabs:

  • Active: Alerts that currently require your attention. You can mark them as read to stop them from flashing, or click Open to view the student.
  • Dismissed: Alerts you have manually hidden by clicking "Dismiss". Use this for alerts you intend to ignore.
  • History: The system automatically resolves alerts when the underlying issue is fixed (e.g., if you update a student's visa expiry date to next year, the "Visa Expiring" alert automatically moves to History).

7. System Settings

Customize the application's behavior to match your workflow using the Settings page.

Security

  • Idle Auto-Lock: Enable or disable the lock screen and choose the timeout delay (5 to 60 minutes).
  • Change Credentials: Update your username or password securely.

Notification Preferences

  • Toggle individual alert types on/off (Visa Expiry, Missing Docs, Backup Reminders).
  • Change the Warning Threshold (e.g., get alerted 60 days before a visa expires instead of 30 days).

Data & Export

To generate comprehensive physical reports, use the Export tool. Select a date range, and the application will generate a formatted Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx) containing all student data within that timeframe.

Data Safety - Backups & Restore
Under the Backup & Restore tab, you can generate a complete snapshot of your database and documents. It is highly recommended to Create a Backup weekly to a USB drive or secure server.

Note: Using the Restore function will permanently overwrite your current system data with the backup file you select. Use with caution.

Audit Log

For compliance and security, the system records every major action taken (logins, record modifications, exports, deletions). Click View System Audit Logs to browse this immutable history.
